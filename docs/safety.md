# Safety
- **Human-supervised only.** Someone must watch the cell whenever the arm has power.
- A **physical stop** (inline e-stop or switched strip) within reach is required before any motion. Test it before first motion.
- No arm power until setup and calibration are done. Support the arm whenever it's unpowered.
- Keep hands out of the arm's reach (~430 mm, plus rail travel) while it moves. Watch for pinch points at the gripper and the rail rack.
- First motions: small, single-joint moves, with your hand on the stop.
- Enclosed printers: doors are opened by hand. Arm door opening isn't supported.
- Don't leave the cell running unattended. The cell runs with human-supervised AI and is not designed for unattended use.
