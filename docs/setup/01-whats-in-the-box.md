# 1. What's in the box

This is the first step of the self-service guide. Tick each item before you go on.

**Safety:** Don't plug anything in yet. Leave the arm's 12 V power supply in the box until [step 4](04-wiring-power-stop-cameras.md).

## Checklist

### Arm and rail
- [ ] Robot arm (seven joints plus a gripper)
- [ ] Linear rail with the arm's carriage on it
- [ ] Printed legs on the rail (one at each end)
- [ ] Two bench clamps

### Power and stop
- [ ] 12 V power supply for the arm (barrel plug)
- [ ] Raspberry Pi power supply (USB-C, 27 W)
- [ ] Red stop button on the 12 V lead, **or** a switched power strip you can reach

### Controller and cameras
- [ ] Small computer (Raspberry Pi 5, or a Pi 4)
- [ ] microSD card (16 GB or larger)
- [ ] Powered USB hub
- [ ] Wrist camera and its printed mount
- [ ] Scene camera (the one that looks at the printer bed)
- [ ] USB cables, and an Ethernet cable if you have one

### Setup extras
- [ ] Setup card, if one came in the box (it has the setup Wi-Fi name)
