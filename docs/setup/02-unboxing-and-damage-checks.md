# 2. Unbox and check for damage

Open the box and look at every part before you mount anything.

**Safety:** Keep the 12 V supply unplugged. Support the arm so it cannot fall or fold under its own weight.

## Look for
- Cracks in printed parts
- Bent rail, loose V-wheels, or a pinion that does not sit on the rack
- Cut or pinched cables
- A missing stop button or power supply
- A loose camera lens or broken USB plug

## If something is damaged
Stop. Take a photo (no faces, serial numbers, or network names) and see [Getting help](../getting-help.md). Do not power the arm.
