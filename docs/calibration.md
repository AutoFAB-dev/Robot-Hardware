# Calibration
Do this after the cell is **Online** in the app, with someone's hand on the e-stop.

1. **Arm zeroes:** follow the arm calibration in the app. **TODO:** link the exact in-app steps.
2. **Rail home:**
   - Power-cycle the 12 V supply with the carriage at the **low end** of travel (the rail drive loses its place when power is cut).
   - With the arm still, push the carriage by hand against its near end stop, then choose **Home rail** in the app.
   - Measure the rail travel with a tape measure and enter it when asked. **TODO:** confirm the app flow.
3. **Wrist camera:** check focus and that the gripper jaws are in view. Re-seat the mount if the view is tilted.
4. **Scene camera:** fix it rigidly with a view of the whole bed. Don't move it after calibration.
5. **Bed fiducials:** print the fiducial tile and holder (**TODO: files**) and follow the in-app placement. The bed height at the removal position is measured here.
6. **First moves:** small, single-joint moves at low speed, with your hand on the stop. Then a first test removal, still with someone at the stop.
